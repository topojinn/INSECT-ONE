# INSECT ONE

## **Operational Checklist/Launch Routine**

**Point 1 | Rocket and Extras Check**
The aircraft body MUST be completely dry. The wings must be fully attached. The lower camera must be active and clean.

**Point 2 | Filling and Pressurizing the Engine**
Fill the water tank properly, without letting it overflow. Connect the air tank correctly and check that the pump hole is clean. Take the pump and attach the outlet to the aforementioned hole in the tank. Fill it to approximately 15 bar. Check the pump pressure gauge for any water leaks or holes.

**Point 3 | Inserting the Snails**
Check the capsule padding. Carefully insert Almaz and Almaz2. Keep a reserve ready in case of problems with the primary pilots.

**Point 4 | Attaching the second stage cables**
Carefully insert the 20-meter cable into the designated hole, keeping the water tank upside down. Make sure there are no holes or cracks not covered by the cap through which water could escape, making stage 2 impossible or severely limited.

**Point 5 | Final check of the water expulsion cable**
The second stage pin must be securely engaged. The cable attached to it must not be tangled. Make sure the cable is securely attached to the base.

**Point 6 | Parachute check**
Check the parachute for any holes and that it opens correctly.

**Point 7 | Departure and departure**
Carefully check and ensure there are no people within a few hundred meters. Engage the first stage rocket and depart.

**Point 8 | Launch**
Rocket launch.

## **Emergency Procedures**

**Error 001 | Water Boost Failed to Initiate**
*Error Definition*: The water release valve pin released correctly, but the rocket still descended.

*Error Reason*: The force of the air and/or the mass of the water relative to the weight of the rocket failed to push it upward.

*Solution*: Move away and initiate landing procedure. Once landed, wait 3 minutes: failure to initiate could indicate the presence of residual water or compressed air.

**Error 002 | Parachute Deployment Failed**
*Error Definition*: The parachute does not deploy despite proximity to the ground.

*Error Reason*: The parachute may have become snagged, the system may have failed, or the velocity may have torn it from the rocket's attachment.

*Solution*: Ensure the second smaller parachute is deployed successfully and proceed anyway. If the second parachute fails, move away immediately. The rocket could fall within a 200-meter radius of the launch pad; stay clear.

**Error 003 | Horizontal Flight/Wind Shear**
*Error Definition*: The rocket is not following its trajectory, but is skidding horizontally in an unintended area.

*Error Reason*: A sudden gust of wind or the rocket is unbalanced.

*Solution*: Do NOT follow the rocket. Wait until it stops completely before approaching.

**Error 004 | Second Stage Cable Break or Malfunction**
*Error Definition*: The 20-meter cable used to detach the second-stage valve has broken or become tangled.

*Error Reason*: Excessive tear-off tension, knot in the skein on the ground, or tangled first stage ascent.

*Solution*: If the cable has torn but the bolt has been removed, exit the aircraft's operating range (200 meters). A final push will be given, and then the parachute will open properly. If the cable has torn without removing the bolt, the parachute will not open, and the two slugs risk being crushed on impact.

**Error 005 | Structural Failure**
*Error Definition*: The cardboard fuselage bends or falls apart during the first stage's thrust or when tearing at 20 meters.

*Error Reason*: Moisture in the material or bending stress from the wind.

*Solution*: The rocket enters a random free fall. Rely on cardboard and internal cushioning for the host Almaz and Almaz2. Protect yourself under something hard, or, if no suitable object exists, shield your face and neck with your arms and hands. Once landed, move away and wait for a complete stop.

**Error 006 | Unknown Landing Parameters**
*Error Definition*: The rocket landed outside the intended 200-meter range in an unseen area (e.g., thick vegetation, water, etc.).

*Error Reason*: A gust of wind or a poorly calculated trajectory.

*Solution*: Do NOT immediately run toward the rocket. Wait 2 to 3 minutes. Approach only after being certain there is no danger.
